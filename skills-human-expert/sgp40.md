---
name: SGP40
description: A digital VOC (volatile organic compounds) sensor.
---

## Operation

- The **Output**: 0-500 VOC Index. The value 100 refers to the average indoor gas composition over the past 24 h. While values between 100 and 500 indicate adeterioration, values between 1 and 100 inform about improvement of the air quality.

- Measurement range: 0-1000ppm.
